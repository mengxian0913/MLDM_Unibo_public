Machine Learning and Data Mining — Claudio Sartori, University of Bologna

Goal of this lab¶

Lab 3 ended with a question: the NordicMart tree chosen by accuracy finds 22 of 108 churners — is accuracy the right number to choose with? Lecture 10b gave the toolbox, Lecture 11 three new classifiers. Today we put both into code, and we end by teaching GridSearchCV to choose with NordicMart's own money.
